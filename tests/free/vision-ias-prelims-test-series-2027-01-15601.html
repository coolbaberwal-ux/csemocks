  1. राजनीतिक न्याय
  2. आर्थिक स्वतंत्रता
  3. प्रतिष्ठा की समानता
  4. राष्ट्र की एकता और अखंडता

Only oneकेवल एक
Only twoकेवल दो
Only threeकेवल तीन
All fourसभी चार

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The Preamble provides:

  • Justice: Social, Economic and Political. (Item 1 is included)
  • Liberty: Of thought, expression, belief, faith and worship. (There is no Economic Liberty; economic dimension is associated with Justice. Item 2 is absent.)

Hence, exactly three objectives (1, 3, and 4) are stated.
