ForumIAS Prelims Test Series (PTS) 2027 — Batch 5 Schedule & Syllabus

ForumIAS 📅 Starts: 19 July 2026 📝 65 Tests (4 Levels) ⏱️ 100 Qs / 2 Hrs

Program Overview

The PTS 2027 Batch 5 comprises 65 tests structured across 4 progressive stages designed to build discipline, test strategy, and comprehensive static & dynamic syllabus coverage.

LEVEL 1

Sectional Mastery

23 GS Sectional + 7 CSAT Tests + 1 Comprehensive Revision based on NCERTs and standard books.

LEVEL 2

Subject Full Tests

6 GS Comprehensive + 4 CSAT Advanced Tests focusing on cross-topic integration and speed.

LEVEL 3

Simulators

10 GS + 9 CSAT Simulator tests calibrated on previous 6–7 years UPSC trend analysis.

CURRENT AFFAIRS

5 Value Addition

Quarterly Current Affairs tests covering 24 months of current developments.
